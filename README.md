# Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis (2025)

## Project Overview

This project analyzes Cyclistic bike-share data from January 2025 to December 2025 to understand how annual members and casual riders use bikes differently.

The analysis explores riding patterns, ride frequency, ride duration, and seasonal trends to identify opportunities for converting casual riders into annual members.

## Business Objective

Cyclistic aims to increase annual memberships by understanding the behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members and developing targeted marketing strategies to encourage casual riders to become members.

## Tools & Technologies

- **MySQL** – Database management and SQL-based data analysis
- **Power BI** – Data visualization and interactive dashboard development

## Analysis Areas

- Monthly riding patterns
- Ride distribution by rider type
- Daily riding activity
- Average ride duration by weekday
- Behavioral differences between casual riders and annual members

## Key Findings

- Approximately **5.55 million rides** were analyzed.
- Annual members account for **63.99%** of total rides.
- Casual riders account for **36.01%** of total rides.
- Casual riders have longer average ride durations than members across all days of the week.
- Riding patterns vary across months and weekdays between the two rider categories.

## Business Recommendations

- Launch membership conversion campaigns during peak riding seasons.
- Introduce weekend-focused promotions to attract casual riders.
- Highlight the benefits of annual memberships to frequent casual riders.
- Develop marketing strategies based on differences in rider behavior.
